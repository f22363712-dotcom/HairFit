# HairFit 90-second demo script

## 0:00–0:15 — problem

“HairFit lets someone preview a hairstyle before sitting in the chair. The goal is to turn an abstract reference into something the client and stylist can discuss.”

Show the generated preview and the uploaded source/reference relationship.

## 0:15–0:35 — photo-conditioned model

“This 3D scene comes from the current fused image through a real reconstruction task. The source image is shown beside the scene, and the hair part is confirmed before editing.”

Show front, left, right and back. Pause briefly on the disclosure that unseen rear geometry is inferred.

## 0:35–1:05 — virtual trim

“The selected hair is represented as model-derived bundles. Clicking a visible bundle shortens its distal section and releases a small detached tip. Reset restores the original reconstructed geometry.”

Click two visible hair areas, wait for the detached tips, then click Reset.

## 1:05–1:20 — stylist handoff

Generate the consultation card and show the regional trim counts.

## 1:20–1:30 — limitation

“The rear shape and physical hair length are estimates. The virtual result is a communication aid, not a promise of individual-fiber simulation or exact salon length.”

Keep webcam pinch out of the main success path. Mention it only if asked as an experimental extension.
